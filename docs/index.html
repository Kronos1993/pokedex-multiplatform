Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Pokedex App, developed and published by CarebridgePro.

EFFECTIVE DATE · JUNE 20, 2026

1. Who We Are

Pokedex App is developed by CarebridgePro ("we," "us," or "our"), based in the United States. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects — and what it does not — and how it functions.

2. Information We Collect

Pokedex App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or any other sensitive device permission, and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs of any kind.

3. Local Data Caching

Pokedex App retrieves Pokémon data (species, abilities, moves, types, and related information) from the public PokeAPI service. To improve performance and reduce unnecessary network requests, responses from PokeAPI are cached locally on your device in a local database for up to 7 days, after which they are automatically refreshed.

This cached data consists entirely of publicly available Pokémon reference data — it does not include any personal or device-identifying information, and it never leaves your device.

4. Third-Party Services

The app retrieves data from PokeAPI, a free, public, third-party API providing Pokémon reference data. These requests do not include any personal or account information — only the data needed to look up Pokémon, moves, abilities, or related game information.

You can review PokeAPI's documentation for more information about the service itself.

5. Data Security

Since no personal information is collected or transmitted by Pokedex App, there is no personal data at risk through use of the app. The locally cached Pokémon data can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Pokedex App is suitable for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
